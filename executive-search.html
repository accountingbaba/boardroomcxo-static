§05 · Questions

Frequently Asked Questions.

What roles does Boardroom CXO search for?

CEOs, MDs and business heads, CFOs and finance heads, CMOs and marketing leaders, sales, retail and business development heads, and CHROs, plus the layer just below the CXO, for companies in India and the UAE.

Which sectors do you work in?

We work across industries. Our searches today fall in five sectors: financial services, including banks, NBFCs, insurers and wealth firms; manufacturing; technology and IT services; consumer and D2C; and retail and jewellery.

How does an executive search with Boardroom CXO work?

In days 1 to 7 we map what the role pays across your category and city, and agree what the role must deliver over the next 12 to 24 months. From day 8 to about day 30 we approach people already doing the job at your competitors, build a confidential shortlist and run interviews. Between day 30 and day 45 we reach the offer, with a realistic uplift, usually 25 to 30%, not the 40 to 50% many companies offer.

What does an executive search cost?

A Rs 10,000 service fee (including GST) starts the search. It is not refundable, and it is adjusted against the final fee of one month's CTC (as defined in our terms of business), invoiced when your hire joins. If the hire leaves within 90 days, we find one replacement free. Searches are not exclusive: you are free to use other agencies on the same role.

Do you run searches in the UAE?

Yes, for senior and CXO roles only. We run searches for companies in Dubai and across the UAE, including India-based brands building a Gulf team. In the UAE, AED 1,000 starts the search. It is not refundable, and it is adjusted against a final fee of one month's salary, invoiced when your hire joins. If the hire leaves within 90 days, we find one replacement free. See UAE search.
