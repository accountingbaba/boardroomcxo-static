§03 · How We Work

A CA-founded team that understands both sides of the desk.

The method is the same as in India: map the pay, agree the brief, approach people in role, and set an offer that closes.

→ 01

Headhunted, Not Advertised

We approach people already doing the job at competitor companies, confidentially. Your company is named only to candidates who choose to go further.

→ 02

UAE First, India-Sourced by Choice

We look at leaders already working in the UAE first. Sourcing from India or elsewhere is available when a client wants it, for example an India-based brand that wants a trusted leader for its Gulf business.

→ 03

Real Pay, Before the Budget

We map what the role pays in the market before you commit a budget, so the offer is neither too low to close nor higher than it needs to be.

→ 04

How the Fee Works

AED 1,000 starts the search. It is not refundable, and it is adjusted against the final fee of one month's salary, invoiced when your hire joins. If the hire leaves within 90 days, we find one replacement free. The first consultation is free.

§04 · Questions

UAE search, answered.

More in our guide to hiring a CXO for a UAE company.

What roles do you search for in the UAE?

Senior and CXO roles only: CEOs, MDs and business heads, CFOs and finance heads, CMOs and marketing heads, sales and business development heads, and CHROs and HR heads.

Who do you run UAE searches for?

Companies in Dubai and across the UAE, including India-based brands building a Gulf team, and consulting, business-setup and corporate service firms.

Do you hire locally in the UAE, or from India?

Both, depending on the role. We look at leaders already working in the UAE first. Sourcing from India or elsewhere is available when a client wants it, for example when the role needs experience the local pool does not have.

What does a UAE search cost?
