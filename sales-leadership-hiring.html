The best regional sales leaders in retail are already running someone else's target.

A National Sales Head who can run a P&L across zones, and a Regional Manager who can actually hold a distributor and store network together, are proven inside a competitor's sales organization, not browsing job boards. Reaching them takes research and a confidential approach, not a job posting.

§02 · Roles We Fill

From the sales floor to the boardroom, one search practice.

A / Store Leadership

Store Manager

  • Store-level P&L, footfall conversion, team leadership
  • The proving ground for every sales leader above this level
B / Area & City Management

Area Sales Manager

  • Multi-store ownership across a city or cluster
  • Sometimes titled Area Business Manager
C / Regional Leadership

Regional Sales Manager

  • Distributor and channel network across a region
  • Sometimes titled Zonal Head
D / National Leadership

National / Zonal Sales Head

  • Full revenue ownership across zones or the country
  • Board-level sales strategy and forecasting

§06 · Questions

Frequently Asked Questions.

What sales roles do you place?

Store Managers, Area Sales Managers, Regional Sales Managers, and National or Zonal Sales Heads, for retail, jewellery, and D2C brands scaling past their first 50 stores or an equivalent revenue stage.

How is this different from a generalist recruitment agency?

We headhunt directly from competitor retail, jewellery, and D2C sales teams rather than sourcing from job boards. The strongest regional and national sales leaders in this category are already running someone else's target; reaching them takes research, timing, and a confidential approach, not a job posting.

Do you source only in India, or internationally too?

Primarily India, sourced from competitor retail, jewellery, and D2C sales organizations. International or NRI candidate sourcing is available when a client specifically wants it.
