§01 · Why This Is Different

The D2C leaders you want are not job-hunting.

Four things decide whether a D2C leadership search works.

→ 01

The best people are not applying

Performance marketers and founder's-office talent who have grown a brand are already inside a competitor's growth team. They have to be approached. Why they are not on Naukri

→ 02

Two different skill sets

Brand building and ROAS-literate performance marketing are separate disciplines. We screen for the blend your stage needs, not a generic marketing résumé.

→ 03

Going offline needs a different leader

A brand opening stores needs someone who has run retail, not only someone who has grown online revenue.

→ 04

Offers built on real pay

A well-paid candidate will usually move on a 25% to 30% uplift on verified earnings, not the 40% to 50% brands assume when they start from the CTC slip. How the gap works

§04 · How We Work

A CA-founded team that understands both the brand and the balance sheet.

→ 01

Headhunted, not sourced

We approach people already doing the job at competing brands, confidentially. The strongest candidates are not applying anywhere, so we do not wait for applications.

→ 02

Real pay, verified

We check what a candidate actually earns, not just the CTC slip, and benchmark the offer on our 2025-26 market mapping by store count and city tier.

→ 03

How fees work

A Rs 10,000 service fee starts the search, and it is adjusted against the final fee of one month's CTC, invoiced when your hire joins.
