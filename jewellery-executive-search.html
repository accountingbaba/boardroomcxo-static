§01 · Why This Is Different

In jewellery, the leader carries the brand's trust.

Four things make senior hiring in jewellery different from the rest of retail.

→ 01

Trust is the product

A jewellery CMO or retail head carries purity, exchange and buyback promises, not just campaigns. Tanishq learned this when it put karatmeters in its stores to test customers' gold in front of them. Read the story

→ 02

Pay is hidden

At legacy jewellery houses a large part of pay sits outside the CTC slip. In our searches the gap typically runs 25% to 35%, and in some cases much more, so we verify real pay before an offer is made. How the gap works

→ 03

Category races move fast

When a new category opens, as lab-grown diamonds have, the question is whether the leadership bench is being built as fast as the funding. Read the Aukera story

→ 04

Promoter-led businesses

Many jewellery houses are family-run or promoter-led. They need leaders who can work with the founder, not around them.

§04 · How We Work

A CA-founded team that understands both the brand and the balance sheet.

→ 01

Headhunted, not sourced

We approach people already doing the job at competing brands, confidentially. The strongest candidates are not applying anywhere, so we do not wait for applications.

→ 02

Real pay, verified

We check what a candidate actually earns, not just the CTC slip, and benchmark the offer on our 2025-26 market mapping by store count and city tier.

→ 03

How fees work

A Rs 10,000 service fee (including GST) starts the search. It is not refundable, and it is adjusted against the final fee of one month's CTC, invoiced when your hire joins. If the hire leaves within 90 days, we find one replacement free. Searches are not exclusive.

→ 04

How long it takes

A CMO or CHRO search typically takes 30 to 45 days to an offer. The start date then depends on the leader's notice period. More on timelines
