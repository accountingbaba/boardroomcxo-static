Frequently Asked Questions.

What is Boardroom Moves?

Our leadership-change announcement series on LinkedIn, published when someone joins a new company in a marketing, sales, BD, strategy, or other CXO-track role. It's a paid placement, reach out and our team will share current pricing.

What is Inside the Boardroom?

A series profiling leaders who have made a real, verifiable impact, particularly in retail. These are editorial picks based on the strength of the story, not a paid placement. Pitch us the story and our team decides based on fit.

Is there a cost to be featured?

Boardroom Moves is a paid placement; contact us for current pricing. Inside the Boardroom is an editorial selection and is not paid.
