§04 · Questions

Frequently Asked Questions.

What is Boardroom Moves?

Our leadership-change announcement series, published on the Boardroom CXO LinkedIn page when someone joins a new company in a marketing, sales, BD, strategy, or other CXO-track role.

What is Inside the Boardroom?

Our long-form series on leaders of well-known Indian brands who turned a business around, made a bold bet that paid off, or built something from nothing, particularly in retail and consumer. Each story is researched and written by our team from public sources, published on LinkedIn and as a full article on boardroomcxo.com. These are editorial picks. Suggest a leader and our team decides based on fit.

What is the difference between Boardroom Moves and Inside the Boardroom?

Boardroom Moves announces a new role, soon after a leader joins a new company. Inside the Boardroom tells the longer story of what a leader at a well-known brand has already achieved. Moves is written from the leader's LinkedIn. Inside the Boardroom is researched from public sources and needs nothing from the leader.

How are features chosen?

For Boardroom Moves, our team reaches out with the options when we see a move. Inside the Boardroom is an editorial selection.
