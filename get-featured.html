Questions

Frequently Asked Questions.

What is Boardroom Moves?

Boardroom Moves announces new senior appointments in India on the Boardroom CXO LinkedIn page, followed by 7,000+ people. It covers leaders who join a new company and leaders promoted to a bigger role in the same company, in CXO-track and senior leadership roles.

How much does Boardroom Moves cost?

The weekly Brief is free. A Feature, your own LinkedIn post with a creative, is ₹1,499 + GST (₹1,769 total). Plus adds an article about you on boardroomcxo.com, an Instagram post and a CXO WhatsApp group invite, for ₹3,000 + GST (₹3,540 total). A Bundle of four Plus features for one company is ₹9,999 + GST (₹11,799 total).

Can I be featured for a promotion in the same company?

Yes. A promotion to a bigger role in the same company is a move, the same as joining a new company. About one in five Moves so far have been internal promotions.

Do I see the write-up before it is published?

Yes. You see the draft first and can ask for changes. Features are then posted on a Tuesday or Thursday.

Will the article show up when people search my name?

The Plus article is a page on boardroomcxo.com with your name, new role and company in its title, listed in our sitemap for Google. Boardroom CXO pages already appear in Google for searches of leaders' names. No one can promise a set position in Google, so we do not.

Can a company announce several new leaders?

Yes. The Bundle covers four Plus features for leaders from the same company, used within a year, for ₹9,999 + GST.

How is this different from a paid press release?

A press release service sends your announcement to a network of news sites. Boardroom Moves posts it to the Boardroom CXO LinkedIn page, followed by 7,000+ people, and with Plus gives you a page on boardroomcxo.com about your move. It does not pitch you to journalists. Our guide to executive PR options in India compares the routes.

How do I get started?

Pick a plan and message us on WhatsApp, or fill in the form on this page. Our team gets back to you within one business day.

What is Inside the Boardroom?

Inside the Boardroom is our long-form series on leaders of well-known Indian brands who turned a business around, made a bold bet that paid off, or built something from nothing, particularly in retail and consumer. Each story is researched and written by our team from public sources, published on LinkedIn and as a full article on boardroomcxo.com. These are editorial picks. Suggest a leader and our team decides based on fit.

What is the difference between Boardroom Moves and Inside the Boardroom?

Boardroom Moves announces a new role, soon after a leader joins a new company or is promoted. Inside the Boardroom tells the longer story of what a leader at a well-known brand has already achieved. Moves is written from the leader's LinkedIn. Inside the Boardroom is researched from public sources and needs nothing from the leader.
