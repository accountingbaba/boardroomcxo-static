How does executive search work?

A search firm maps pay, defines the role, then quietly approaches people already doing the job at competitors.

Read the answer →

How much does a CXO search cost in India?

With Boardroom CXO, one month of the hire's CTC: Rs 10,000 (including GST) to start, adjusted against the final invoice when the hire joins.

Read the answer →

How long does it take to hire a CMO, CFO or CHRO?

About 8 weeks to a confidential shortlist with Boardroom CXO; the start date then depends on the notice period.

Read the answer →

What is the difference between retained and contingency search?

Retained search is paid at the start and exclusive; contingency is paid only on a hire. We charge Rs 10,000 (including GST) upfront, the rest on joining.

Read the answer →

Executive search firm vs recruitment agency vs job portal: what is the difference?

A portal waits for applicants, an agency sends CVs of people looking, a search firm approaches leaders who are not looking.

Read the answer →

How does a confidential executive search work?

Candidates are approached without your company being named; only those who choose to go further learn who it is.

Read the answer →

How do you hire a CXO for a UAE company?

Decide local or India-sourced, check pay against real placements, then approach people already doing the job.

Read the answer →

What is CXO hiring?

Recruiting for the top roles, such as CEO, CFO, CMO and CHRO, usually through a confidential direct approach.
